Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

PT Connect Network operates a provider-discovery and marketing platform. This policy describes, at a high level, information we may collect through provider applications, website forms, analytics, and account or billing services.

Information we collect

Provider applications may include clinic name, contact name, email, phone, website, city, state, ZIP code, specialties, and plan selection. We may collect technical information such as browser/device data and site activity for security and analytics.

How we use information

We use information to operate the platform, review provider applications, communicate about services, process subscriptions, provide directory functionality, improve marketing performance, and maintain security.

Payments

Payments and subscription billing are processed by Stripe. PT Connect Network does not need to store full payment-card numbers on its own servers.

Healthcare information

Do not submit diagnoses, detailed medical histories, treatment records, or other sensitive health information through general website forms unless the platform specifically provides a secure workflow designed for that information.

Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help operate the website, database, communications, analytics, and billing systems. Provider information approved for publication may appear in the public directory.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact PT Connect Network through the contact information provided on the website.
