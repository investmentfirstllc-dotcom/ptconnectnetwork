FAQ

Questions patients and clinics ask.

Does PT Connect Network provide medical advice?

No. The platform is designed for provider discovery and marketing. Clinical diagnosis and treatment decisions belong to licensed healthcare professionals.

Does being listed guarantee an appointment?

No. Availability, insurance acceptance, treatment suitability, and scheduling are determined by each participating clinic.

How do clinics participate?

Clinics can request information about provider-network participation and marketing services. Any commercial arrangement should be reviewed for applicable federal and state healthcare compliance requirements.

How is the platform paid?

The intended business model is marketing and patient-acquisition services rather than payment for healthcare referrals. Specific pricing and compensation structures should be reviewed by qualified healthcare counsel before launch.
